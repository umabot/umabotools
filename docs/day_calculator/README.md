# Date Calculator

Date Calculator is a single-page browser tool for comparing two dates and understanding the span between them at a glance. It breaks the range into total days, weeks, business days, and weekend days, and it also shows a visual timeline so you can see where the selected dates fall relative to today.

## What It Does

- Calculates the number of days between two chosen dates.
- Breaks the range down into weeks, business days, and weekend days.
- Shows a visual timeline to help place the dates in context.
- Runs entirely in the browser as a self-contained HTML page.

## Usage

Open [day_calculator.html](../../day_calculator.html) in your browser, choose the two dates you want to compare, and review the detailed breakdown and timeline summary.
